You choose how much it sees

The camera is optional.
Always.

Before every drive you pick an analysis mode. Telemetry-only never opens a camera at all, and you still get a full debrief. Mirror-check analysis runs on your phone — facial footage is never uploaded.

  • Pick your mode per drive — nothing is locked in.
  • Front-camera analysis happens on-device using Apple's Vision framework.
  • Uncertain checks are yours to confirm, from a clip that never leaves your phone.
Read the privacy policy →
Telemetry only Sensors and GPS. No camera is opened at any point during the drive.
Telemetry + road view Adds the rear camera for positioning, following distance and hazards ahead.
Telemetry + road + face view Adds on-device mirror-check detection as you approach turns and junctions.
