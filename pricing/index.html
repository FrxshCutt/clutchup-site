Plans are only available inside the ClutchUp app. This page exists so you know what to expect before you download. All billing is handled by Apple through your App Store account. The 1- and 3-month plans renew until you cancel (any time, in your device's App Store subscription settings); the 6-month plan is a one-off purchase that does not auto-renew — it runs its course and ends.

Paying for a learner?

Most ClutchUp subscriptions are bought by a parent for a learner. What you're buying is feedback on the practice you already supervise: after every drive, the app writes up what went well and what needs work, so private practice between paid lessons stops being guesswork. It supports lessons — it doesn't replace an instructor.

If auto-renewal is the thing you hate, the 6-month plan is the one to look at: a single payment that covers a typical learning journey and then simply ends. The purchase is made inside the app on the learner's iPhone through Apple — using Family Sharing's Ask to Buy, you can approve it from your own phone.

Before you pay anything

Free means genuinely free.

The free tier isn't a countdown: record unlimited drives and get a score for every one of them, forever, with no card details anywhere. What Pro adds is the AI feedback — the written breakdown of what happened on each drive and what to work on next.

  • Unlimited recorded drives and unlimited scores — free, always.
  • AI feedback on each drive is the paid feature.
  • Nothing is charged unless you choose a plan inside the app.
Buying Open ClutchUp, go to Upgrade, pick a plan. Apple's native payment sheet handles the rest — Face ID or your Apple ID password.
Managing Everything lives in your Apple ID subscription settings: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → ClutchUp.
Cancelling Cancel the 1- or 3-month plan in the same place, any time — Pro stays active to the end of the period you've paid for. The 6-month plan needs no cancelling: it never renews.
